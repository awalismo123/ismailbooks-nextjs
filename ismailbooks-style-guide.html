1. Storefront color palette

Midabbada storefront-ka waa inay noqdaan kuwo diirran, nadiif ah, oo la aamini karo. Oxblood waa midabka iibka. Navy waa midabka kalsoonida. Gold waa accent yar oo kaliya.

5. Typography

Madaxa: Fraunces ama Playfair Display. UI/body: Inter ama IBM Plex Sans. Reader: Lora ama Source Serif. Qoraalka reader-ka waa inuu ahaadaa 18px–20px, line-height 1.7–1.8.

Fraunces / Playfair Display

Inter — UI text, buttons, navigation, labels, dashboard, forms.

Lora — Reader text. Qoraalka buugaagta waa inuu noqdaa mid deggan, diirran, oo fudud in la akhriyo muddo dheer.

Element Font Size Line height
Hero H1 Fraunces 40px–60px 1.05–1.1
Section H2 Fraunces 28px–36px 1.15
Card title Inter / Fraunces 20px–24px 1.25
Body text Inter 16px–18px 1.65
Reader text Lora 18px–20px 1.75–1.85
Meta / caption Inter 13px–14px 1.5

7. Payment & dashboard states

User-ku waa inuu had iyo jeer ogaadaa xaaladda iibkiisa: pending, approved, rejected, ama locked preview.

Sugaya ansixin Pending

9. Design rules

Xeerarka muhiimka ah ee naqshadda IsmailBooks.

Do

  • Isticmaal paper background oo diirran.
  • Isticmaal oxblood primary CTA ahaan.
  • Isticmaal navy links iyo trust ahaan.
  • Isticmaal gold accent yar ahaan.
  • Isticmaal green success/approved kaliya.
  • Isticmaal buttons waaweyn mobile-ka.
  • Isticmaal reader themes oo deggan.
  • Isticmaal high contrast text.

Don’t

  • Ha isticmaalin dark glass storefront ahaan.
  • Ha isticmaalin purple/blue neon gradients.
  • Ha isticmaalin gold primary button ahaan.
  • Ha isticmaalin white text gold korkiisa.
  • Ha isticmaalin pure black reader background.
  • Ha isticmaalin pure white long reading background.
  • Ha ka dhigin bogga mid aad u midab badan.
  • Ha ku qarin paid content client-side kaliya.
